# JIGSAWS demo clips

These endoscopic recordings are samples from the **JHU-ISI Gesture and Skill Assessment Working Set (JIGSAWS)**, collected at Johns Hopkins University with the da Vinci surgical system (Intuitive Surgical).

| File | JIGSAWS task | Subject | Trial | Capture |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `suturing.avi` | Suturing | D | 005 | capture1 |
| `knot_tying.avi` | Knot tying | D | 005 | capture1 |
| `needle_passing.avi` | Needle passing | D | 005 | capture1 |

Citation: Gao, Y., Vedula, S. S., Reiley, C. E., Ahmidi, N., Varadarajan, B., Lin, H. C., Tao, L., Zappella, L., Béjar, B., Yuh, D. D., Chen, C. C. G., Vidal, R., Khudanpur, S., & Hager, G. D. (2014). *JHU-ISI Gesture and Skill Assessment Working Set (JIGSAWS): A surgical activity dataset for human motion modeling.* MICCAI Workshop.

Use is for research and demonstration of technique metrics, not clinical evaluation.
